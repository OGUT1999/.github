Registration applications are being prepared; an unregistered mark is still protected against passing off (s. 7).

## The code licence gives no right to the marks

- **Apache-2.0** (our libraries) says so in s. 6: it « does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor », except to describe the origin of the work.
- **FSL-1.1-ALv2** (our products, [ADR-012](docs/adr/ADR-012-licensing-libraries-open-products-fair-source.md)) says so in its « Trademarks » clause.

You may copy, fork and modify the code under its licence. You may not use our names or birds as **your** brand.

## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your work **uses**, **is built on** or **is compatible with** a Krizaka library or product (« built with Krizaka UI », « an Orazaka pack »), in plain text, without our logo and without suggesting we endorse you.
- Keep our names in the copyright, NOTICE and licence files of a fork, as the licence requires.
- Write about us (articles, reviews, talks, tutorials), with the names and, for illustration, our unmodified logos.
- Publish a package under your own scope that targets ours (`yourname/krizaka-ui-theme`), when its description says it is not an official Krizaka package.

## What needs our written permission

- A product, a service, a company, a domain name, a package scope (`@krizaka`), an app-store name or a social account whose name **contains** or **imitates** one of our marks.
- A **fork distributed or hosted under our name**: rename it and remove our logos and birds before you publish it (a fork is welcome; a confusing one is not).
- Our logos or birds on merchandise, in advertising, or modified.
- Any wording that suggests Krizaka certifies, sponsors or supports your work (« official », « certified », « Krizaka partner »).
