# Internal audit — template

> ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.2; ISO/IEC 27701. Status: template, **à valider par l'avocat** for the legal references. A filled audit is kept in the private evidence folder; only its findings become public issues, without personal information.

Yearly, and before any certification audit. The auditor does not audit their own work: in a one-person company, an outside reviewer, or a documented self-assessment with the compensating controls named.
