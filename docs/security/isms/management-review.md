# Management review — template

> ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.3. Status: template, **à valider par l'avocat** for the legal references. The filled review is kept in the private evidence folder.

Yearly, and after a serious incident.

## Inputs

1. Actions from the last review.
2. Changes (products, suppliers, laws, the lawyer's answers on .github#250).
3. Incidents of the year ([register](../../privacy/incident-procedure.md#the-register)) and drills.
4. The internal audit's findings ([template](internal-audit.md)).
5. The SoA: controls still `gap` or `partial` ([`soa.json`](soa.json)).
6. The risk register and its treatment ([`risk-register.json`](risk-register.json)).
7. Restore tests and continuity ([`continuity.md`](continuity.md)).

Including: approval of the [security policy](security-policy.md), acceptance of residual risks, resources, and whether to certify (.github#284).
