# Data processing agreement — public template

> Template without company data, for B2B clients where Krizaka is the service provider (P-39.1 art. 18.3). **à valider par l'avocat.** Filled copies with the parties' details are private (`~/Documents/krizaka/docs/legal/`).

1. **Parties and roles.** The Client is the controller; Krizaka is the service provider and processes personal information only on the Client's documented instructions.
2. **Subject, nature, duration.** <service>; <categories of data>; <categories of persons>; for the term of the service.
3. **Purpose limitation.** No use for another purpose, no sale, no profiling.
4. **Confidentiality.** Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality.
5. **Safeguards.** Measures proportionate to sensitivity (P-39.1 art. 10): access control, encryption in transit and at rest, logging, backups, secure development; described in the ISMS.
6. **Sub-processors.** Listed in an annex; the Client is told of any change.
7. **Transfers outside Québec.** Only after an assessment of the privacy factors (art. 17), written agreement, and the Client's information.
8. **Incidents.** Notice to the Client without delay after Krizaka becomes aware of a confidentiality incident, with the information the Client needs for arts. 3.5-3.8 (see the incident procedure).
9. **Rights requests.** Krizaka helps the Client answer within the 30-day period.
10. **Retention and destruction.** Data is returned or destroyed at the end of the service, or at the Client's request, and the destruction is confirmed.
11. **Audit.** The Client may obtain the information needed to check compliance.
12. **Law and language.** Québec law; French version first (Charter of the French language, arts. 55, 57).
