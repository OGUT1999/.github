# Rights-request workflow

> Good-faith version of P-39.1 (Law 25), **à valider par l'avocat**.

Requests: access (art. 27), rectification (art. 28), deletion or de-indexing and portability (art. 28.1), withdrawal of consent. Received at the privacy address published on each site, or from the account settings.

| Step | What | Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Receive | Log in the request register: date, product, type. Acknowledge. | 2 business days |
| 2 Verify | Confirm the requester's identity with the least data possible. For a processor role, forward to the client who is the controller and tell the requester. | 5 days |
| 3 Decide | Grant, partly grant or refuse with the legal ground (arts. 38-41 exceptions). | 20 days |
| 4 Answer | In writing, in French first, with the right to ask the Commission d'accès à l'information to review (art. 32). A refusal is reasoned. | **Within 30 days of receipt** (art. 32) |
| 5 Keep | Keep the request and the answer for the time the law requires; a refused request is kept until the review period ends. | |

Tracked as data with the same rule as the incident register: date received, due date (received + 30 days), status, answered on. A late request is a finding in the monthly review.
